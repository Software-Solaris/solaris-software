AI usage in Solaris Software

We know that AI is used almost everywhere, all the time. We encourge our developers NOT to use AI. We have tried it and we think the risk and the time lost is not worth the time. The reasons are:

  • Solaris Software is a university project. People should join to acquire knowledge, not for a machine to do code they don't understand. If that is the case, you can do it yourself at home and leave the project. When you ask an AI to find a solution to a problem, you are shortcutting knowledge. Looking on the internet, comparing various sources, seeking different solutions is a good exercise for your mind, even if that takes you x20 more time. Your brain needs time to process information. We never hurry our developers on doing features as fast as possible. We want them to understand the problem and find a creative solution. If we wanted to hurry our software development, the more experienced people on the team will tackle that.

  • Using AI gives you brain fog, literally! You get bombarded with changes you don't understand, endless permisisons to execute random commands on your computer. All of this to do the job your were supposed to do. What's the point on that? We get some students or people want to join the teams for more recognition on their CVs to later chase the carrot on the job market. What sometimes they miss is that you cannot shortcut knowledge. You can easily distinguish if someone did something with AI or not, same as if they lied in their CV or not. Save yourself some headaches and do it yourself. Fighting a problem and finding a solution you made is a pleasurable experience, really!

  • You are responsible of what you do. If you create some lines of code that and AI made, then you are responsible for those lines. If AI is a black box with a behaviour you don't understand...so are the lines, unless you review them entirely. Even in that case, maybe it was better doing it yourself on the first time.

We will encourage our developers not to use AI for the software development, and we will always ask them to explain code to us that we might suspect they don't understand fully their behaviour. AI should be a tool. Develop accordingly.
